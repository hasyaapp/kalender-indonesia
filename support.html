Tanggalan support

Found a wrong date or holiday, or need help? Email asharyhsm@gmail.com. Please include the date, what the calendar shows, and what you expected.

Questions

  • Where do the holidays come from? The public holidays and joint leave days (cuti bersama) of the SKB 3 Menteri. For years without a decree, the calendar shows estimated holidays and says so in its footer. New decrees are added to this website and picked up by the app without an update.
  • Why can a Hijri date differ by a day? Hijri months are calculated with the MABIMS criteria used by the government, or with Muhammadiyah's KHGT. The government fixes Ramadan, Syawal and Zulhijah by sidang isbat; shift the Hijri dates (−2 to +2 days) to match it.
  • Which Javanese and Chinese calendars are used? Sultan Agung's Javanese calendar with its windu and kurup cycles (Asapon since 1936), and the Chinese lunisolar calendar on China Standard Time, which gives the Imlek and Cap Go Meh dates.
  • Can I change how it looks? Yes. Every number and label is normal Canva text inside a group, so you can change fonts and colours, move or delete items. The app also offers presets, grid lines, shaded holidays, four colours, and Indonesian, English or Arabic.
