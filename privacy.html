Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy covers the Tanggalan app for Canva ("the app"), published by Aksara Nusantara.

What we collect

Nothing. Calendars are calculated inside your browser. Your choices are not sent to us or to any third party, and the app has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and no cookies of its own.

Holiday data

When it opens, the app downloads the latest list of public holidays from this website (data/libur.json), which is hosted on GitHub Pages. The request contains no information about you or your design; GitHub may log it as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Canva

The app runs inside Canva. Calendars you add to a design are stored by Canva as part of that design, under Canva's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this changes, we will update this page before the new version of the app is released. Questions: asharyhsm@gmail.com.
