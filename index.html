Tanggalan

A Canva app that adds an editable Indonesian calendar: one month, a whole year with one page per month, or a 12-month poster. Each day can show its Hijri date, Javanese pasaran and date, and Chinese lunar date, with the public holidays and joint leave days from the SKB 3 Menteri.

Hijri months follow the MABIMS criteria (government) or Muhammadiyah's KHGT. Special days include national days, other Islamic days, sunnah fasts, Galungan, Kuningan and Cap Go Meh. The calendar can be written in Indonesian, English or Arabic.
